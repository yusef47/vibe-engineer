  needs a display: `gui_required` on `--serve`. To be validated on a GUI
  workstation.
- DWG read/write (only DXF was exercised; DWG fixtures would be the next
  fixture set, still synthetic).
- Real-world drawings, layers with locked/frozen states, block tables with
  attributes, XREFs, layouts/plotting beyond the audit — future milestones
  per `PLAN.md`.
- The assistant-panel TCP protocol (`127.0.0.1:8766`) — its service side is a
  later phase; the `--serve` channel is the verified automation surface.
